Source Health

Each automated source check reports its real status. Large SEC bulk datasets remain separate where hosted-runner access is restricted.

Data discipline: “Daily updated” means the website checks official sources daily. Ownership datasets publish on different schedules, so every section retains the latest real observation date instead of presenting stale values as today's holdings.
Official sources include U.S. Treasury Fiscal Data and the Monthly Treasury Statement, Treasury International Capital, Federal Reserve H.4.1 and Financial Accounts, New York Fed SOMA and Primary Dealer Statistics, SEC Form N-MFP and SEC Form N-PORT.
