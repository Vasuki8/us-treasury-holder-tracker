The All range combines Treasury-specific annual survey observations before the monthly TIC series begins with the complete monthly history from 2011 onward. Pre-1994 survey columns are not used because they report broader debt rather than Treasury debt separately. TIC country attribution can reflect custodian location rather than the ultimate beneficial owner.
Data discipline: the site checks official sources daily, but ownership, maturity, TGA, auction, buyback, dealer, average-rate and interest-expense datasets publish on different schedules. Every displayed value keeps its real observation date; ownership shares preserve source valuation caveats; tentative buyback caps are distinguished from actual purchases; and forward variable/inflation-linked interest remains labeled as estimated.
Official sources include U.S. Treasury Fiscal Data (Debt to the Penny, MSPD, Daily Treasury Statement, Treasury Securities Auctions, Average Interest Rates and Interest Expense), TreasuryDirect buyback schedules, Treasury International Capital, Federal Reserve H.4.1 and Financial Accounts, and New York Fed primary-dealer statistics.
