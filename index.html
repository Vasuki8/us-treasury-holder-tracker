Foreign Holder History

The All range shows the complete stored monthly TIC history for the selected country; Treasury-specific annual survey history remains available in the country detail drawer. TIC country attribution can reflect custodian location rather than the ultimate beneficial owner.
Data discipline: the site checks official sources daily, but ownership and maturity datasets publish on different schedules. Every displayed value keeps its real observation date, and variable/inflation-linked future interest is labeled as estimated rather than presented as fixed.
Official sources used by the retained dashboard include U.S. Treasury Fiscal Data (Debt to the Penny and MSPD), Treasury International Capital, Federal Reserve H.4.1 and Financial Accounts, with historical Treasury survey data used for country history.
