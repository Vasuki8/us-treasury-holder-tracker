Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective September 15, 2026

Cancel anytime

When recurring subscriptions are activated, you may cancel at any time to stop the next renewal. Unless otherwise stated at checkout, access continues through the end of the billing period already paid for.

Refunds

Subscription charges are generally non-refundable after a billing period begins, except where required by law, for duplicate or clearly erroneous charges, or where we approve a refund because the paid service was materially unavailable. Any refund approved through the payment processor will be returned to the original payment method where possible.

Annual plans

Annual plans are offered at a discounted effective monthly rate in exchange for yearly billing. Canceling an annual plan prevents the next yearly renewal but does not normally create a prorated refund for unused months in the current paid year.

API usage

Data API fees, once activated, may reflect reserved capacity, authentication, rate limits and infrastructure usage. API charges are generally non-refundable after the applicable billing period begins unless required by law or a documented billing error occurred.

How to request billing help

Do not post card numbers, bank information, passwords or other sensitive billing data in a public GitHub issue. Once checkout is enabled, billing receipts and the customer billing portal will provide the appropriate private support path. Until then, the project’s issue tracker can be used for general product questions only.
