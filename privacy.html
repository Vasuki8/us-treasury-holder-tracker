Privacy Policy

Effective September 15, 2026

1. Public dashboard

The public dashboard can be used without creating an account. The current site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub and the networks used to deliver the site may process standard technical information such as IP address, browser type, request time and requested pages under their own privacy practices.

2. Browser storage

The site currently uses browser local storage for lightweight preferences such as the selected monthly or annual billing view. This information stays in your browser unless a future account feature explicitly synchronizes it.

3. Payments

When paid checkout is activated, payments will be processed by Stripe or another clearly identified payment processor. The tracker should not receive or store your full payment-card number. The payment processor may collect identity, billing, payment and fraud-prevention information under its own privacy policy.

4. Accounts, alerts and API access

When authenticated features are launched, we may collect the minimum information needed to operate them, such as email address, subscription status, saved alert rules, delivery preferences, API-key metadata and usage records. This policy will be updated before those features collect additional personal information.

5. How information is used

Information associated with paid or authenticated features may be used to provide access, send requested alerts and briefs, prevent abuse, enforce rate limits, troubleshoot problems, measure product performance and meet legal or accounting obligations.

6. Sharing

We do not sell user data to advertisers. Information may be shared with service providers that are necessary to operate the product, such as hosting, payment, authentication, database, email and monitoring providers, or when disclosure is legally required.

7. Retention and security

We aim to retain account and operational information only as long as needed for the service, security, billing, legal or accounting purposes. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Sensitive credentials and payment secrets will not be intentionally embedded in public GitHub Pages JavaScript.

8. Your choices

You can clear local browser storage at any time. Once accounts are launched, instructions for requesting access, correction or deletion of applicable account data will be provided through the product’s authenticated support flow, subject to legal retention requirements.

9. Changes and contact

This policy may change as Treasury Pro, alerts and the Data API launch. The current version and effective date will remain posted here. For product or policy questions, use the project’s support issue tracker. Do not post passwords, payment-card numbers or other sensitive personal information in a public issue.
